Advertising Policy

This Advertising Policy explains how AstraSpin ("we," "us," or "our") handles advertisements on our website https://astraspin.in/.

1. Third-Party Ad Networks

We use third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense, GamePix, and GameMonetize, to display ads on our platform. These networks may use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

2. Ad Placement

We strive to place ads in a way that does not interfere with your gameplay experience. Ads are typically displayed in designated banner areas or as interstitials between game sessions. We do not use misleading ad placements that mimic game buttons or controls.

3. Ad Content

We do not control the specific ads that are displayed, as they are served by third-party networks. However, we rely on these networks to filter out inappropriate content. If you encounter an ad that you believe is inappropriate or misleading, please contact us.

4. User Responsibility

Your interaction with advertisements on our platform is at your own risk. We are not responsible for the content of these ads or any products/services they promote.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Advertising Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
