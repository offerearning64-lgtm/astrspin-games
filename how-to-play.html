How to Play

How to Play Games on AstraSpin

Playing games on AstraSpin is easy and fun! Follow these simple steps to start playing your favorite games instantly.

1. Browse Games

Visit our All Games page to see the full collection of games available on AstraSpin. You can also browse by Categories to find specific types of games.

2. Search for Games

If you are looking for a specific game, use the search bar on the All Games page. Type the name of the game or a keyword, and the results will update automatically.

3. Start Playing

Once you find a game you want to play, simply click or tap on the game card. The game will open in a fullscreen player. Wait for the game to load, and then you can start playing immediately.

4. Game Controls

Most games on AstraSpin can be played using your mouse, keyboard, or touchscreen, depending on the game and your device. Some games may require a combination of controls. Instructions are usually provided within the game itself.

5. Exit a Game

To exit a game and return to the AstraSpin website, simply click or tap the back button in the top left corner of the screen or use your browser's back button.

Tips for the Best Experience

  • Use a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) for the best performance.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
  • If a game is not loading, try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache.
  • For mobile devices, play in portrait or landscape mode as recommended by the game.
