🪙 Coins & Rewards

What are AstraSpin Coins?

AstraSpin uses virtual Coins to make the gaming and reward experience simple to understand. Users can collect Coins through eligible activities like spinning the wheel or claiming daily bonuses.

Coin Value:
1,000 Coins = ₹1
5,000 Coins = ₹5
10,000 Coins = ₹10
20,000 Coins = ₹20

How can I collect Coins?

  • Spin the Wheel: Play the AstraSpin game to earn coins. Rewards range from 0 to 5,000 Coins per spin.
  • Daily Rewards: Claim your daily bonus every 24 hours. Maintain a 7-day streak to earn up to 2,000 Coins on Day 7!
  • Play Games: Keep playing to unlock more opportunities.

How does Redeem work?

Once you reach the minimum redemption threshold, you can request a payout or claim a gift.

Minimum Redeem:
20,000 Coins = ₹20

Coins are virtual reward points and are not automatically equivalent to cash unless a valid redemption is approved and available.

Gift Store

Use your Coins to claim real gifts directly from the Gift Store inside the Settings menu.

  • Browse available items (Max value ₹100).
  • Tap "Claim" on an item you can afford.
  • Confirm your claim to generate a unique Claim ID.
  • Contact us via WhatsApp to complete the fulfillment process.

⚠️ Important Device Notice

Your Coins and progress are stored locally on this device/browser using the current device storage system.

If you change, reset, or lose your device/browser data, we cannot guarantee that your previous Coins or progress will be recoverable.

Please keep this in mind before changing or resetting your device.
